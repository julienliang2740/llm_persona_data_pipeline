# Research notes (template)

Optional but recommended. Record here:

1. **Verified** — claims from your research report you checked against the sources, with passage ids.
2. **Corrected** — where the report was wrong and what the source actually says.
3. **Unverifiable** — what you could not check and therefore did not rely on.
4. **Left open** — interpretive choices you deliberately did not resolve (mirror them in
   `unresolved_choices` with a `working_assumption`).
5. **Implications for the pipeline** — anything this tradition needs that the generic prompts may
   not supply (e.g. "the asker's own state is the variable that changes the answer").
