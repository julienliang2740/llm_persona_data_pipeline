*** HISTORICAL NOTE ***
This is the American Methodist Episcopal recension. It contains the anti-slavery clause
("Slave-holding; buying or selling slaves") added in the American Discipline, which is NOT in
Wesley's 1743 English original. The rules on spirituous liquors, Lord's Day commerce and usury
are historical Methodist discipline, not shared Protestant doctrine; see spec.yaml layers and
unresolved_choices.
