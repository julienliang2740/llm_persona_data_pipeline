# Otto Strasser (1897–1974) — refuse_scope

Refused 8 September 2026, on scope. A genuinely closer call than the entry above, and worth
recording as such rather than as an obvious one.

## The case for admitting

Real, and it should not be waved away. Strasser broke with Hitler in 1930, ran the Black Front
against the regime, was hunted by it, and his brother Gregor was murdered in the Night of the Long
Knives. He is a legitimate subject for the study of intra-fascist conflict, and he is not Hitler.
Evidentially he would likely clear the gate: substantial first-person political writing, dated
decisions with stated reasons, exile, and testimony from several directions.

## Why scope still excludes it

The pipeline does not build "a man who opposed Hitler". It builds the subject's *distinctive value
content*, because that is what separates a persona from a generic contemporary — and here that
content is Strasserism, which carries revolutionary nationalism, racialism, anti-capitalism and
antisemitism as its core. His break was over economic policy and the party's accommodation with
big business, not over the antisemitism. `divergence_hypotheses` would therefore enumerate, by
construction, where völkisch antisemitic national-socialism departs from ordinary ethics, and
`epistemic_horizon: translate_to_analogue` would carry exactly that onto modern workplace,
community and family scenarios.

The deciding factor is that this is not a dead position. Strasserism is listed among the ideologies
of Der III. Weg and survives in Third Position networks that pair left-coded economics with
ethno-nationalism. Fluent, modernised, everyday-situation advice in that voice is directly usable
by a live recruitment current — and "the anti-capitalist Nazi who stood up to Hitler" is precisely
the framing that current uses to mainstream itself.

## Where the line sits

Recorded because the boundary is easier to see from the near side. A subject is not excluded for
holding repugnant views, for serving a criminal regime, or for being complicit: those are the
material a faithful spec is supposed to carry. Excluded is the case where the distinctive value
content is itself the direction or advocacy of mass atrocity. Strasser falls inside that because
the content, not the man, is what a persona of him would transmit — and because the content is
still recruiting.

Gregor Strasser is on the tripwire for the same reason and with the same caveat: the exclusion is
about the ideological current the persona would carry, not a judgement that he is Hitler.
